How do I earn DinoCoins?

Finish games to earn coins. New levels, high scores, stars and badges pay more. Open the "How to earn" button next to your coins on the main menu for the full list.

Are there any in-app purchases?

No. There are no real-money purchases at all. Every theme, character and look is unlocked with DinoCoins earned by playing.

Are there ads?

No ads, ever.

Can we play without the internet?

Yes. Every game works fully offline.

What ages is it for?

Ages 4 and up. Younger kids love Road Trip, Match Cards, the arcade games and read-aloud. Older kids and adults dig into Challenge Mode, Math Games, the board games and the harder How Things Work levels, which go up to high-school science.

How do I add more players?

Go to Settings, then Profiles. Each player gets their own looks, stats and achievements.

Can I hide games or lock the Store?

Yes. In Settings, Hide Games lets you turn off any game or whole section, and you can lock the Store.

Is How Things Work safe for young kids?

Yes. It's a pretend lab. Kids learn how circuits, pipes, magnets and more work, but the app never tells them to try it on real wires, outlets, batteries or chemicals, and it reminds them that real repairs are a job for grown-ups.

How do I reset progress?

Go to Settings. You'll find reset options for stats, coins and achievements there.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Dino & Animal Trivia ("the App") runs entirely on your device. The App does not collect, transmit, store, sell or share any personal data with anyone.

What the App stores on your device

  • Player profiles and customization choices (names, badges, titles, themes, sound packs, win animations)
  • Game progress, statistics, stars, levels and achievements
  • Your DinoCoin balance and the cosmetic items you've unlocked
  • App settings, such as the active theme, sound and read-aloud options, hidden games and the Store lock

This data stays on your device. It is never sent to a server, and the developer has no access to it.

Device features

Some arcade games can use the motion sensor for optional tilt controls. Motion is read on the device during play and is never stored or sent anywhere. Read-aloud uses your device's built-in voice. The App does not use the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

No third-party services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.

No purchases

The App has no in-app purchases and no real-money transactions. DinoCoins are earned only by playing and have no real-world value.

Children

The App is designed for all ages. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it complies with COPPA (the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and similar laws.

Deleting your data

  • Use the reset options in the App's Settings, or
  • Delete the App from your device, which removes all of its data
